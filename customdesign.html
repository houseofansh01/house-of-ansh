How It Works

01
Share Your Vision

Message us on WhatsApp with your idea — a sketch, a reference image, a description, or even just a feeling. Nothing is too vague to start with.

02
Design & Quote

We sketch your piece and share a design for your approval along with a quote. We refine until it's exactly what you envisioned — no extra charges for revisions.

03
Crafting Begins

Once approved and confirmed, our artisans begin handcrafting your piece in pure .925 sterling silver. You receive progress updates along the way.

04
Delivered to You

Your piece is packed in our signature box and shipped Pan India with full tracking. Ready to be worn, gifted, or treasured.

Pricing

Custom pieces are priced based on design complexity, silver weight, and finish. We always share a full quote before any work begins — no surprises.
