The Name

अंश — Ansh

From Sanskrit aṃśa, Ansh means a part, a fragment, a share — a quiet piece of something whole. In spiritual thought, it is a fragment of light, a portion of the divine.

Beside silver, it becomes poetry: a part of the moon, a share of purity, a piece of grace.

House of Ansh is where jewellery becomes an ansh of you — not worn as decoration, but carried as identity. A fragment of your story, made timeless.

A part of the moon. A piece of light.
Jewellery that becomes a part of you.

About Us

Built on a belief

“Jewellery should make a woman feel something before it makes her look good.”

House of Ansh was founded in March 2026 by a mother and daughter with a simple belief: luxury should feel intimate, not distant. Built with intention, honesty, and a quiet respect for what you wear — and what it comes to mean.

We do not create jewellery only for occasions. We create it for the everyday ritual of choosing yourself — in small moments, in new chapters, in becoming.

Every piece that leaves our hands is one we would wear ourselves, gift with pride, and keep for years. That is the only standard we believe in.
